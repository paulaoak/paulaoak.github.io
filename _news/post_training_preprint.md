New preprint out on [Uncertainty-Aware LLM Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27747). **TLDR**: Introduces a scalable framework that replaces standard single-point LLM post-training with an ensemble of LoRA adapters, enabling explicit uncertainty quantification and model specialisation.
